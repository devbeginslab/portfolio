About me

Strategist, builder, teacher at heart.

I'm Oluwaseun Ogunseye, known as Seunfunmi, a Lagos-based digital entrepreneur and media buyer. I'm currently the Media Buyer Supervisor at DeeSpark Global Services, where I lead Meta ad campaigns. Beyond that, I run sellIT.ng, a digital marketing and e-commerce agency helping Nigerian brands sell online.

My background spans public administration, business administration, teaching and entrepreneurship. That mix lets me connect strategy, systems and people: from campaigns and sales funnels to the CRMs and tools that run a business behind the scenes.

I also founded the Feed a Child Initiative, a grassroots programme giving children in rural Nigerian communities digital and technical skills.
